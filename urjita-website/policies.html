Privacy Policy

Urjita Yog ani Pranic Upchar Kendra (“Urjita”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we handle it.

What this website collects

This website has no database and no accounts. The enquiry form does not send anything to a server — it composes a message and opens WhatsApp on your own device, so you decide whether to send it. Your language preference is stored in your own browser and never leaves it.

What we collect when you contact us

When you message, call or email us, we receive whatever you choose to share — typically your name, phone number and a description of your concern. If you attend a session, we may keep brief notes to provide continuity of care.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange sessions.
  • To send workshop dates and meditation timings, if you have asked to be added.
  • To maintain our own records of the service provided.

Confidentiality

Anything you share in a session is treated as confidential. We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not share it with third parties except where required by law, or where there is a serious and immediate risk to someone’s safety.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and links out to WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps. Those services have their own privacy policies and their own handling of your data once you leave this site. Our online store, where it exists, runs on a separate domain with its own policy.

Testimonials and photographs

We publish client messages and photographs only with permission, and we remove identifying details unless you have agreed otherwise. If you would like something taken down, tell us and we will remove it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, ask to be removed from our WhatsApp updates, or ask us to delete your records. Email urjitapranichealing@gmail.com and we will act on it.

Children

Healing for anyone under 18 is arranged through a parent or guardian, who is present or informed throughout. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

We would rather reschedule you than keep money you feel was not well spent. Please read this before booking.

Healing sessions

  • Reschedule free of charge with at least 24 hours’ notice.
  • Cancel with at least 24 hours’ notice for a full refund of anything paid in advance.
  • With less than 24 hours’ notice, or if you do not attend, the session fee is not refundable — though we will do our best to accommodate genuine emergencies.
  • Once a session has been given, including a distant healing, it cannot be refunded.

Session packages

Where sessions are booked as a package, unused sessions may be refunded on request, less the standard single-session rate for each session already taken. Packages are personal to you and cannot be transferred without our agreement.

Workshops and courses

  • Cancel seven or more days before the start date for a full refund of your fee.
  • Cancel between two and six days before, and you may carry your fee to the next batch, or receive a refund less any non-recoverable costs such as printed manuals and certification charges.
  • Within 48 hours of the start date, or after the course has begun, fees are not refundable. You may nominate someone else to take your place with our agreement.
  • If we cancel or postpone a workshop, you receive a full refund or a place on the next batch, whichever you prefer.

Free sessions

Weekly meditations and moon sessions are free of charge, so nothing is payable and nothing is refundable. Timings may change; we announce this on our WhatsApp list.

Wellness products

Products are sold through our separate online store, which carries its own returns and refund policy. Where products are bought directly from us in person, unopened items in original condition may be exchanged within seven days. For reasons of hygiene, opened bath salts and sprays cannot be returned.

How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are returned by the same method you paid, normally within seven to ten working days. To request one, message +91 84463 39272 or email urjitapranichealing@gmail.com with your name, the date of booking and what you would like us to do.
