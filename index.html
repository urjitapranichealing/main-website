What is Pranic Healing

An ancient science, made practical.

Pranic Healing uses prana — life energy — to support physical, emotional and psychological wellbeing. It is a no-touch, no-drug technique that works by cleansing and energising the body's energy field.

In Indian Yoga Shastra this energy body is called the Pranamaya Kosha, and its vital energy centres are the chakras. By balancing these centres, Pranic Healing supports the body's own capacity to restore harmony.
