What is Pranic Healing

An ancient science, made practical.

Pranic Healing uses prana — life energy — to support physical, emotional and psychological wellbeing. No touch, no medicines: it works by cleansing and energising your energy field.

Indian Yoga Shastra calls this energy body the Pranamaya Kosha, and its vital centres the chakras. Balance those centres and the body’s own capacity to restore harmony gets to work.
