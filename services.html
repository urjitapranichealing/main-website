One-to-one sessions

Healings for the whole of you.

No touch. No drugs. Nothing to swallow or apply. A session works entirely with your energy body — cleansing what is congested and energising what is depleted — while your medical care continues exactly as your doctor advises.

01

Physical & Psychological Wellbeing

In personDistant

Support for the body and the mind together — stress, anxiety, low mood, sleep difficulty, fatigue, and general recovery. Kshipra's psychology training means the emotional layer is never treated as an afterthought. This work runs alongside your medical or therapeutic treatment, never in place of it.

02

Relationship Healing

FamilyCouples

For strained bonds — between partners, parents and children, siblings, or colleagues. The work focuses on clearing the emotional charge you carry, so that conversations that once turned into arguments have room to go differently.

03

Financial & Business Healing

Business ownersPremises

For entrepreneurs and professionals feeling stuck. Includes pranic feng-shui for homes, shops and offices — clearing the energy of the space itself so it supports the work happening inside it.

04

Pranic Facial & Facelift

No touchBest in a series

A gentle, non-invasive protocol working with the energy of the face and skin. Most people take it as a short series rather than a single sitting, and many pair it with the stress relief work.

05

Healing for Children

With parentsPranic Psychotherapy

Kshipra's specialisation. Drawing on both School Psychology and Pranic Psychotherapy, she works with children who are anxious, restless, or struggling at school — and with the parents holding it all together. Parents are guided too, never left outside the room.

06

Distant Healing

AnywhereBy appointment

You do not have to be in the room. Distant sessions are arranged by appointment and are the usual choice for clients outside Badlapur and Dombivli, or for anyone unwell enough that travelling is difficult.

How a session works

Simple, and entirely up to you.

01

We talk first

A short conversation about what brought you here and what you have already tried. Nothing is assumed.

02

You rest

You sit or lie down, fully clothed. Kshipra works in your energy field, a little distance from the body.

03

You go home with something

Simple practices to keep between sessions — breathing, salt baths, or a short meditation.

Session fees vary by the type and length of healing. Message or call and Kshipra will share the current fees, along with an honest view of how many sessions you are likely to need.
