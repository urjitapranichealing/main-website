Kshipra Lokare

MCKS Pranic Healing Instructor · Associate Certified Pranic Healer · Counseling Psychologist

Kshipra came to this work through the mind. Studying psychology in 2012, she met Pranic Healing — and it answered a question her textbooks had left open: why some people heal and others, with the same care, do not.

By 2014 she was practising in earnest. Pranic Psychotherapy gave her a way to reach children and their parents that conversation alone could not. Children who could not name their distress began to settle. Parents who felt helpless found something they could do.

Today she heals and teaches — training others as a Certified MCKS Instructor, while continuing one-to-one work from both centres.

Madhavi Lokare

Yoga Teacher · Acupressure Therapist · Energy Healer

Madhavi Lokare has been teaching yoga for twenty-five years and practising acupressure for more than twenty-five — long enough that many of her students are now the parents of her students. For the past fifteen years she has worked with energy healing alongside both.

Three disciplines, one habit of attention: the breath, the pressure point, the energy field. People come to her with stiffness, fatigue and the ordinary wear of a working life, and stay for the steadiness.

She anchors Urjita’s group practice — the weekly meditations and the yoga-based sessions that many people meet the centre through.

The lineage

Grand Master Choa Kok Sui

Modern Pranic Healing was founded by Grand Master Choa Kok Sui, who turned the ancient wisdom of Yoga into a practical, systematic method. His research showed that balancing the energy body and its chakras supports health, well-being and prosperity.

Everything taught at Urjita follows the MCKS system as it was set down — no improvisation, no dilution.

Your body has an incredible natural ability to heal itself. Pranic Healing simply activates and enhances what is already there.
