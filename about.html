The story

Psychology first. Then prana.

Kshipra came to this work through the mind. While studying psychology in 2012 she was introduced to Pranic Healing, and something in it answered a question her textbooks had left open — why some people heal and others, with the same care, do not.

By 2014 she had begun practising in earnest. Her commitment deepened through Pranic Psychotherapy, which gave her a way to reach young clients and their parents that conversation alone could not. Children who could not put words to their distress began to settle. Parents who felt helpless found something they could do.

Today she both heals and teaches — training others as a Certified MCKS Basic Pranic Healing Instructor, while continuing one-to-one work from her centres in Badlapur East and Dombivli East.

The lineage

Grand Master Choa Kok Sui

Modern Pranic Healing was founded by Grand Master Choa Kok Sui, who transformed the ancient wisdom of Yoga into a practical, systematic method. His research showed that balancing the energy body and its chakras supports health and wellbeing.

Everything taught at Urjita follows the MCKS system as it was set down — no improvisation, no dilution.

Your body has an incredible natural ability to heal itself. Pranic Healing simply activates and enhances what is already there.
